-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fonts
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Flow carries ten fonts for its old machines' themes. Seven are other
people's, whose notices follow; Flow Teletype, Flow Instruments and Flow
notaTopaz are Flow's own.

VT323 2.000, a video terminal's font (the Green screen and Amber screen themes)
    Copyright 2011, The VT323 Project Authors (peter.hull@oikoi.com)
    License: SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (text below)
    Source:  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/vt323

Bedstead 3.261, after the Mullard SAA5050 teletext character generator (the
School computer theme)
    By Ben Harris. The program that generates Bedstead and all of its newly
    designed glyphs have been released into the public domain; its author
    believes the original SAA5050 bitmap font to be essentially in the public
    domain in the United Kingdom.
    Source:  https://bjh21.me.uk/bedstead/

Gallant Raster Term 1.007, the Sun workstation console's Gallant font (the
Workstation theme)
    Copyright (c) 2025, Jens Schweikhardt
    License: BSD 2-Clause License (text below)
    Source:  https://github.com/NanoBillion/gallant

SGI Screen, the Silicon Graphics workstation's Screen font (the Graphics
workstation theme), its 18-pixel bitmaps made outlines for Flow
    Copyright (c) 2003 Silicon Graphics, Inc.
    License: MIT License (text below)
    Source:  the SGI ProPack's fonts, as converted by Nicholas Riley,
             http://sabi.net/nriley/software/screen/

Flow Teletype 1.000, after the type of a Teletype Model 33 (the Teletype
theme)
    Copyright 2026 the Flow authors
    License: MIT, as Flow is
    Drawn for Flow by Flow's authors, and part of Flow; not another party's
    work.

Flow Instruments 1.000, an early-eighties home computer's letters of 8 by 8
dots (the Flow Instruments Home Computer theme)
    Copyright 2026 the Flow authors
    License: MIT, as Flow is
    Drawn for Flow by Flow's authors, and part of Flow; not another party's
    work.

Flow notaTopaz 1.000, a 16-bit home computer's letters of 8 by 8 tall dots
(the Flow notanAmiga CLI theme)
    Copyright 2026 the Flow authors
    License: MIT, as Flow is
    Drawn for Flow by Flow's authors in the manner of the Amiga's Topaz,
    and part of Flow; no glyph is taken from Topaz or any other party's
    font.

Special Elite 1.001, a typewriter's letters
    Copyright (c) 2010 by Brian J. Bonislawsky DBA Astigmatic (AOETI)
    License: Apache License, Version 2.0 (text below)
    Source:  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/apache/specialelite

unscii-8 and unscii-16, bitmapped fonts after 8-bit home computers' and PCs'
letters (the Home computer and Personal computer themes)
    By Viznut. Public domain (or CC0); the GPL unscii-16-full, which contains
    parts of GNU Unifont, isn't carried.
    Source:  https://github.com/viznut/unscii

Copyright 2011, The VT323 Project Authors (peter.hull@oikoi.com)
